# Transactional ICP, Pain Points & AI Plays

A second VERA audience, distinct from the current verticals (coaches, consultants,
creatives who sell judgment and "run on trust"). These ten sell **throughput**:
per-transaction, paperwork-heavy, deadline-driven service businesses.

**Positioning fork:** the hook flips from "protect your authenticity" to
**"close more deals, drown in less paperwork."** Same agentic AI, different
emotional driver. For the compliance-gated trades, VERA's human-in-the-loop /
guardrails stance is the *selling point*, not a disclaimer: AI drafts and
organizes, the licensed human signs.

---

## The shared pattern (what we actually solve)

1. **Document / data wrangling**, pulling data out of forms, statements, photos,
   and PDFs into their system. The universal time sink.
2. **Counterparty chasing**, the deal stalls waiting on a missing doc, a
   signature, a callback. They are the human follow-up engine.
3. **Repetitive artifact generation**, the quote, report, itinerary, estimate.
   Same shape every time, rebuilt from scratch.
4. **Follow-up that leaks revenue**, quotes never chased, past clients never
   re-touched. In per-transaction work, a forgotten follow-up is a lost commission.

---

## Tier 1, Lead with these (highest pain, highest AI fit, lowest compliance drag)

### Realtors
- **Pain:** lead follow-up is the whole game and where they're worst (leads cold
  in hours); listing descriptions; CMAs; transaction-coordination checklists;
  sphere-of-influence nurture; showing scheduling.
- **AI play:** instant lead response + long-tail nurture sequences, listing copy
  in their voice, CMA drafts, transaction checklists, content.
- **Caveat:** none material. Highest-fit on the list.

### Contractors
- **Pain:** estimating/bidding eats nights and weekends; quotes go out late and
  never get followed up; change orders and invoicing slip; supplier coordination;
  permit paperwork.
- **AI play:** estimate drafts from site-visit notes/photos, automatic quote
  follow-up, scheduling/dispatch comms, invoice + change-order generation, review
  requests after a job.
- **Caveat:** none material. The money is in the follow-up they don't do.

### Home inspectors
- **Pain:** the report is the bottleneck, photos + findings into a formatted,
  defensible doc, often same-day; plus winning repeat agent referrals.
- **AI play:** report drafting from field notes/photos, scheduling, agent-
  relationship nurture, client comms.
- **Caveat:** a human owns the findings; AI formats and drafts, does not judge.

## Tier 2, Strong fit, low compliance friction

### Travel agents
- **Pain:** itinerary building and supplier research (flights/hotels/visa rules)
  is hours per trip; quotes; client changes; rebooking comms; commission tracking.
- **AI play:** itinerary and quote drafts, research synthesis, change/rebooking
  comms, follow-ups.
- **Caveat:** low liability, clean fit.

### Bookkeepers
- **Pain:** transaction categorization, reconciliation, chasing receipts,
  month-end close, client reporting, catch-up work.
- **AI play:** categorization, receipt capture, reconciliation assist, client-
  ready report drafts, client chase sequences.
- **Caveat:** accuracy, human reviews before anything ships.

## Tier 3, Compliance-gated (AI drafts/organizes, the licensed human signs)

> This is where VERA's guardrails positioning is the pitch: "your license stays
> in control."

### Tax preparers
- **Pain:** seasonal crush (Jan–Apr); endless document chasing (missing W-2s,
  1099s); data entry from docs into tax software; year-round nagging for docs;
  extensions; IRS notice responses.
- **AI play:** document intake/OCR + categorization, client chase sequences,
  drafting replies to IRS notices, organizing the shoebox.
- **Caveat:** cannot give tax advice or sign returns, that's the human.

### Mortgage brokers
- **Pain:** collecting and re-collecting borrower docs (paystubs, bank statements,
  tax returns); chasing "conditions"; status updates to borrowers and agents;
  lender/program matching.
- **AI play:** doc checklists + intake, borrower/agent status updates, rate/program
  research, pre-qual drafts.
- **Caveat:** TILA/RESPA, nothing AI says can misstate terms.

### Insurance agents
- **Pain:** quoting across carriers; policy comparison; renewals; COIs
  (certificates of insurance); claims hand-holding; cross-sell; follow-ups.
- **AI play:** quote-comparison drafts, renewal reminders, instant COI generation,
  claims-status comms, lead follow-up.
- **Caveat:** licensing, AI can't bind coverage or advise.

### Appraisers
- **Pain:** comp research and report narratives against deadlines; AMC/lender comms;
  public-records research.
- **AI play:** comp/public-records research synthesis, narrative drafts, scheduling
  and status comms.
- **Caveat:** USPAP + appraiser independence, AI never touches the valuation
  judgment. That's the liability core.

---

## Tier 4, Narrow scope (AI helps the business, not the regulated act)

### Notaries (mobile / loan signing)
- **Pain:** the notarial act is in-person and regulated and cannot be automated.
  The business around it bleeds time: scheduling, travel routing between signings,
  invoicing, chasing repeat work from title companies.
- **AI play:** scheduling, route optimization, invoicing, referral/repeat-business
  follow-up.
- **Caveat:** smallest surface area; AI touches only the ops around the act.

## Recommendation

Lead the new segment with **realtors, contractors, and home inspectors**, highest
pain, highest AI fit, lowest compliance drag, largest populations. Roll out the
compliance-gated trades second with the "your license stays in control" framing
front and center.
