# VERA Customer Avatar, Realtors

The single ICP for VERA. Marketing and site copy should speak to this person.
"Everyone" is not specific enough; this is who we attract.

## B. Who is this service for?

**Realtors.**

- **Age:** 25-35
- **Income:** ~$200k/year
- **Lifestyle:** Materialistic. Wants nice cars, wants to outshow the
  competition. Status- and ego-driven.
- **Their problem:** Client follow-ups and leaving money on the table. Lack of
  productivity, signatures, personalized outreach, market research, home
  research, code/compliance research. They HATE administrative work, that's why
  they became realtors. They like people and want to get back to that, and that
  means they make more money.
- **Urgency:** Major. They live and die by productivity. Some months $60k,
  others $0.
- **Emotional state:** On edge, heightened depending on the success of that month.
- **What they care about most:** People (relationships), which also means more
  money.

**Avatar paragraph:**
> This service is for Realtors aged 25-35 who want an edge on the market and to
> gather more clients to live a more frivolous lifestyle. They want their
> administrative work (contracts, signatures, follow-ups) handled while they are
> out and about. They want to be the best in their area; market and home
> research will keep clients happy and their pockets and ego full.

## C. What are they struggling with before contacting us?

So the ads make them feel understood.

- **The problem they're dealing with:** Follow-ups slipping, money left on the
  table, drowning in admin instead of being with people.
- **What they're scared of:** A slow month, and looking bad to their clients.
  (Good reputation = recurring relationship.)
- **What they're frustrated by:** Contracts, compliance, market research, lack
  of awareness.
- **What they've tried before:** ChatGPT, assistants.
- **What happens if they wait too long:** Lose the competitive edge, leave money
  on the table, burnout, loss of client momentum.
- **What they secretly want solved:** (TBD, fill in.)

## Messaging hooks (derived)

- Lead with the **edge / be the best in your area**, and the **money left on the
  table** by slow follow-up.
- Name the **hatred of admin** and the promise of **getting back to people**.
- Speak to the **slow-month fear** and **reputation** (recurring relationships).
- They've tried ChatGPT and assistants; position VERA as the step beyond those,
  built around how they actually work, not another tool to babysit.
