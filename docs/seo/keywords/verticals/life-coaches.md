Rationale: Strongest overlap between commercial intent (coaches evaluating tools) and informational intent (coaches discovering what's possible). Competitors like Elephas, Paperbell, and Luisa Zhou are actively ranking on this phrase, confirming the SERP is live and monetizable. The phrase maps cleanly to VERA's positioning, building the AI stack for coaches rather than replacing them. It also serves as a natural hub for linking to workflow-specific landing content.

## Long-Tail FAQ Questions

These map to high-intent "question" queries life coaches type verbatim. Use as FAQ schema blocks on `/for/life-coaches` and as individual blog post titles.

1. **What are the best AI tools for life coaches in 2026?**
   Intent: Commercial comparison. High volume, high competition. Use as primary FAQ #1 and standalone blog post. Answer should reference VERA's done-for-you build vs. a DIY tool list.

2. **How can I automate my life coaching business without losing the personal touch?**
   Intent: Commercial with objection-handling. Life coaches' #1 stated fear is depersonalization. This captures the concern directly and positions VERA's agent design philosophy. Use in FAQ and blog.

3. **What's the ROI of automating client onboarding for a life coaching practice?**
   Intent: Informational / transactional. Coaches respond to hard numbers (34% fewer no-shows, 3–5 hrs/week reclaimed). Use in FAQ schema and a case-study-style blog post.

4. **How does AI handle mid-week check-ins for coaching clients?**
   Intent: Informational. Addresses a specific workflow coaches want to offload. Use in FAQ and workflow section H3. Ideal for a short-form demo video page.

5. **Can AI write session recaps and follow-up emails for life coaches?**
   Intent: Informational / transactional. Session documentation is the top time drain coaches cite. Directly maps to one of VERA's signature workflows. Use in FAQ and workflow breakdown section.
