## FAQ Long-Tail Questions

Worth answering on the Boca Raton page, each targets a real question pattern visible in SERP PAA boxes and competitor FAQ sections:

1. **What does an AI consultant in Boca Raton actually do for a coach or solo consultant?**
   Targets "AI consultant near me" + "what is" informational entry-point. Bridges awareness to hire intent. Answer should name the specific deliverables (proposals, follow-ups, content, meeting notes) and distinguish coaching from generic tool sales.

2. **What is the difference between an AI consultant and an AI agency in Boca Raton?**
   Targets searchers comparing options. Positions VERA's 1:1, trust-first model against the agency/automation-shop category (AutomateNexus, HummingAgent, AutoCraft). Clear differentiator for VERA.

3. **How much does an AI consultant cost in Boca Raton or Palm Beach County?**
   High commercial intent. Competitors openly advertise $2,500 assessments, $297–$497/month retainers, $5k–$50k+ builds. VERA can answer honestly without a hard price, directing to an intro call, but the question must be answered or the page loses the comparison-shopping visitor.

4. **Do you offer in-person AI consulting or coaching in Boca Raton?**
   Local-intent qualifier. VERA is actually based in Boca Raton, this is a genuine competitive advantage. Answering it concretely (yes, Mizner Park corridor, etc.) reinforces LocalBusiness signals and earns trust from buyers who want a real local.

5. **What is agentic AI, and why does it matter for my Boca Raton consulting practice?**
   Awareness + differentiation. "Agentic AI" is undersupplied in local competitor content, none of the Boca Raton-specific pages use the term prominently. This FAQ earns the informational visitor and sets VERA apart from generic automation agencies.
