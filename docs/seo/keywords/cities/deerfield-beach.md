## FAQ-Style Long-Tail Questions

These map to real "people also ask" patterns observed in South Florida AI consulting SERPs. Use as structured FAQ schema entries on the page.

1. **What does an AI automation consultant in Deerfield Beach actually do for a coach or consultant?**
   Target: coaches and consultants unclear on what they're buying; high conversion value when answered clearly.

2. **How much does AI automation cost for a small business in Deerfield Beach?**
   Target: bottom-of-funnel buyers doing price comparison. Competitors openly advertise $2,500–$15,000 project ranges; VERA can differentiate on scope and accountability.

3. **Can an AI consultant in Deerfield Beach automate my client onboarding and follow-up?**
   Target: coaches and service businesses with high-touch CRM needs, the most common first automation use case in this segment.

4. **What's the difference between hiring an AI agency vs. an AI consultant in Broward County?**
   Target: informational buyers comparing options; creates a positioning opportunity for VERA's boutique, advisory-first model vs. high-volume agencies.

5. **How long does it take to set up AI automation for a Deerfield Beach business?**
   Target: time-sensitive buyers and people evaluating commitment. Competitors advertise 2–4 week timelines; answering honestly differentiates.
