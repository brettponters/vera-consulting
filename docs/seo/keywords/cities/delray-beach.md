## FAQ-Style Long-Tail Questions

These map to actual user search questions and should appear as FAQ schema on the page or as H3-anchored body sections.

1. **How much does AI automation cost for a small business in Delray Beach?**
   Target: cost-intent searchers. Answer with a transparent range and link to a free strategy session CTA.

2. **Can an AI consultant help my coaching business in Delray Beach?**
   Target: coaches specifically. Bridges the coach/consultant niche VERA serves to local search.

3. **What is the difference between an AI consultant and an AI automation agency?**
   Target: mid-funnel comparison searchers. Positions VERA's boutique, hands-on angle vs. churn-and-burn agencies.

4. **How long does it take to automate workflows for a solopreneur?**
   Target: timeline-concerned solopreneurs. Answer: days to weeks, not months.

5. **What AI tools do consultants in South Florida actually use?**
   Target: informational / tool-aware searchers who self-identify as consultants. Natural entry point for trust-building content; bridges to Make, Zapier, Claude, ChatGPT workflow examples.
